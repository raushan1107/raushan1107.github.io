Who is Raushan Ranjan?

Raushan Ranjan is a Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) and Iconic Trainer at Koenig Solutions, delivering Microsoft technology training globally. With over 11 years of experience in education and technology training, he brings deep technical knowledge and a clear teaching philosophy to every session.

Raushan's expertise spans the full Microsoft technology stack — from cloud architecture on Azure and low-code development on Power Platform, to AI engineering with Azure OpenAI, enterprise development with ASP.NET Core and Blazor, and specialized GPU programming with OpenGL and Vulkan for high-performance applications.

Raushan's philosophy is direct: "Why before How." Every training he delivers begins with business context and purpose before diving into implementation. His personal maxim — "Sweat in the right direction brings Peace, Money, and Respect" — defines his approach to education and continuous learning.

What is the Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) credential?

The Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) is Microsoft's premier instructor certification. It authorizes the holder to deliver official Microsoft Learning content globally. To qualify, trainers must demonstrate both technical certification and instructional excellence, and the credential must be renewed annually.

FinMan AI is an AI-powered personal financial intelligence platform created by Raushan Ranjan. It features: a FastAPI + Azure OpenAI GPT-4o backend; an 8-step financial onboarding flow that profiles the user's financial situation comprehensively; a proprietary "Shock Absorber" buffer system that models financial resilience across 9 life risk categories (job loss, medical emergency, family crisis, etc.) using a 3-layer buffer architecture; Excel export/import for financial data; and a warm, professional navy/ivory/amber UI theme.

What is Buddy AI?

Buddy AI is Raushan Ranjan's personal AI assistant — a voice-first agent built with Python, Azure OpenAI (via DefaultAzureCredential), ElevenLabs TTS, and Porcupine wake-word detection. Buddy AI is designed as a personal productivity assistant without external banking API connections by design.
