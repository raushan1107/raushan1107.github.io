Who is Raushan Ranjan?

Raushan Ranjan is a Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT), Founder & CEO of RR Skillverse, and one of India's leading enterprise technology educators. With over 11 years of hands-on training experience, he has shaped the careers of more than 3,400 professionals across 25+ countries.

Raushan's expertise spans the full Microsoft technology stack — from cloud architecture on Azure and low-code development on Power Platform, to AI engineering with Azure OpenAI, enterprise development with ASP.NET Core and Blazor, and specialized GPU programming with OpenGL and Vulkan for defence and high-performance applications.

He founded RR Education in 2014 to democratize access to world-class IT training. Eleven years later, that vision evolved into RR Skillverse — a comprehensive learning ecosystem featuring AI-powered tools, enterprise courseware, and community-driven upskilling.

Raushan's philosophy is direct: "Why before How." Every training he delivers begins with business context and purpose before diving into implementation. His personal maxim — "Sweat in the right direction brings Peace, Money, and Respect" — defines his approach to both education and entrepreneurship.

What is the Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) credential?

The Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) is Microsoft's premier instructor certification. It authorizes the holder to deliver official Microsoft Learning content globally. To qualify, trainers must demonstrate both technical certification and instructional excellence, and the credential must be renewed annually.

What is FinMan AI?

FinMan AI is an AI-powered personal financial intelligence platform created by Raushan Ranjan. It features: a FastAPI + Azure OpenAI GPT-4o backend; an 8-step financial onboarding flow that profiles the user's financial situation comprehensively; a proprietary "Shock Absorber" buffer system that models financial resilience across 9 life risk categories (job loss, medical emergency, family crisis, etc.) using a 3-layer buffer architecture; Excel export/import for financial data; and a warm, professional navy/ivory/amber UI theme.

What is Buddy AI?

Buddy AI is Raushan Ranjan's personal AI assistant — a voice-first agent built with Python, Azure OpenAI (via DefaultAzureCredential), ElevenLabs TTS, and Porcupine wake-word detection. It features: DeepFace emotion detection to adapt responses based on the user's mood; Microsoft Graph integration for Outlook and Teams access; SQLite-backed persistent memory for long-term context; and a manually maintained private knowledge base. Buddy AI is designed as a personal productivity assistant without external banking API connections by design.
